Agentic B2B commerce · working prototype

A buying agent that knows each customer's price, follows the business rules, and hands off when a human should decide

Kestrel Supply is a fictional ERP-integrated B2B distributor. The assistant covers the buyer journey (find products by spec or SKU, account-specific pricing and stock, reorders, carts, quotes, trade terms) and the seller journey (quote requests and handoffs reviewed on a seller desk). The LLM decides what to do; deterministic code decides what is true and what is allowed.
